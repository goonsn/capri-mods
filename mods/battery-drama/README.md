# Low Battery Drama

On a laptop, Capri gets more dramatic the lower your battery goes, and cheers when you plug in.

- Kind: code mod
- Made by: Issei
- Needs: Capri 0.8.6 or newer

| Battery | She says |
|---|---|
| 30% | Heads up, 30 percent battery. |
| 20% | Down to 20. I don't love this. |
| 10% | 10 percent! This is not a drill. (and glitches) |
| 5% | 5 percent... tell the others I was cool. (and lies down) |
| 2% | Goodbye, cruel world. 2 percent. (and glitches) |
| plugged in at 4% | You plugged me in at 4?! My heart. Thank you. (and gives you a finger heart) |
| full | Fully charged. You can unplug me now. |

Ask "how's the battery" or "am I charging" any time. Each step happens once per charge. If the battery drops past several at once, she only reacts to the lowest. Plugging in starts the steps over, and the closer it got, the bigger the cheer: at 10% or less she gives you a finger heart, and above that she dances. The finger heart needs Capri 0.9.0 or newer and her Motion setting on. Without it she dances for that one too. She also knows your battery level when you chat. On its Options page you can turn off any step, and turn off lying down at 5%. On a PC without a battery it does nothing, and says "No battery here. I run on the wall." if you ask. Windows only.
