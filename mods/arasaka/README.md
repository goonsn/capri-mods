# Arasaka

Capri in Arasaka black and red, with the tree emblem on her chest.

- Kind: skin
- Made by: Issei

Her body becomes glossy black armour, lit from the upper left, with her panels as slightly lighter plates. Her hair goes Arasaka red and keeps its shape, her green mask becomes a bone-white faceplate, and her eyes glow red. The white tree emblem sits on her chest in every pose and only shows where her body does. Her glitches go red and black, and her white flash goes red.

Arasaka and its tree logo are from Cyberpunk 2077 (CD Projekt). This is a fan skin.
