# MaxTac

Capri in NCPD MaxTac gear, with a dark helmet and green lens clusters for eyes.

- Kind: skin
- Made by: Issei

Her body becomes a black tactical suit, lit from the upper left, with her panels as armour plates. Her green panels turn into plates edged in a thin violet light strip, and her hair goes dark gunmetal with faint violet flecks. Her green mask becomes a dark helmet, and each of her eyes a cluster of three green lenses. Her glitches stay her own violet and green, which already fit, and her talking mouth matches the helmet.

MaxTac is from Cyberpunk 2077 (CD Projekt). This is a fan skin.
