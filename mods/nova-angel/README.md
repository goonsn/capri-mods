# Nova - Angel

A skin for Capri: an angel with soft wings and a halo, in whatever colour you like. AI generated, moves just as Capri does.

- Kind: skin
- Made by: Chilled (chillteas on discord)

She arrives in violet with a rose halo; then pick her colours on the Mods page. The Edges button colours her whole figure, body, robe and wings together. The Hair button colours her halo. The Body button colours her fine dark lines. White works like any other colour: Edges set to white and Hair to a pink gives a white angel with a pink halo. Accents and Eyes do nothing on her.

Every pose is her own picture: standing, walking, sitting on a window with her legs swinging, lying down, sleeping, dancing to music, and her face for calls and the game HUD. Her motion is Capri's own, traced limb by limb from Capri's animations, so she breathes, swings her legs and dances on Capri's timing.

There are no glitch frames in this skin. Her glitch clips hold the calm pose, arriving and leaving fade in and out, and waking is a soft glow. Capri's own screen tear and shatter effects still play.

She keeps Capri's original stand (the pre-0.8.6 one), which Capri 0.8.6 and later still accept. Needs Capri 0.8.4 or later.

The art is AI generated from imagine.art. Free to use and share with Capri, not to sell.
