# Sleep Timer

Puts your PC to sleep or shuts it down later, with a warning first.

- Kind: code mod
- Made by: Issei

| Say | She says |
|---|---|
| sleep timer 40 minutes | Okay. I'll put your PC to sleep at 10:40 PM, in 40 minutes. |
| shut down at 1am | Okay. I'll shut your PC down at 1:00 AM, in 3 hours. |
| snooze | Fine, 15 minutes more. |
| cancel the sleep timer | Cancelled. Your PC stays on. |

She warns you at 5 minutes and 1 minute. The shutdown isn't forced, so unsaved work can still stop it. A timer missed while the PC was asleep is dropped. Windows only.
