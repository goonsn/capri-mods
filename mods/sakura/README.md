# Sakura

Capri in cherry blossom, head to toe.

- Kind: skin
- Made by: Issei

Her hair goes from deep rose at the roots to pale pink at the tips, dotted with little blossoms that stay put on her, and petals let go of it and drift down with a sway. Her limbs are dark bark with hot-pink panels and leaf-green accents, lit from the upper left so she isn't flat. Works in every pose.

It covers her new standing loop from Capri 0.8.6, facing either way, with its blink and glitches. With Legacy stand on, she wears it on her old stand instead.
