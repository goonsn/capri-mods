# Neon Sign

Capri as a neon sign.

- Kind: skin
- Made by: Issei

Thin glowing tubes run along every part of her (pink hair, cyan body, green panels, yellow eyes) on a dark board that the tubes light up softly, with a glow around her. Now and then a tube buzzes and dims for a frame, and when she glitches the whole sign shorts out. Works in every pose.
