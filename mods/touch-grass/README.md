# Touch Grass

Counts your gaming time and roasts you when it's time for a break.

- Kind: code mod
- Made by: Issei

| Say | She says |
|---|---|
| *(after 2 hours)* | 2 hours of Elden Ring. Go touch grass. I'll guard your save file. |
| how long have I played today | Today: 4 hours. Most of it Elden Ring 4 hours. |
| playtime this week | Last seven days: 14 hours 52 minutes. ... |
| touch grass after 90 minutes | Okay. I'll roast you after 1 hour 30 minutes of gaming. |
| *game* isn't a game | Okay, I won't count it as gaming. |

It spots games installed through Steam, Epic, Riot, Xbox, GOG, Ubisoft and EA, plus a few others. It only reads program paths and window titles, and keeps playtime in its own folder. Windows only.
