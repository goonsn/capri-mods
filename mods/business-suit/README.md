# Business Suit

Capri in a sharp navy business suit.

- Kind: skin
- Made by: Issei

Her body becomes navy suit cloth, with her panels as lighter facets of it, lit from the upper left so it has some shape. At her neck there's a white collar between dark lapels and a long tie in her red, the only red on her clothes. Her hair, face and eyes stay her own. The collar and tie sit on her chest in every pose and only show where her body does, so when her hair hangs in front of her you'll see less of it.

It covers her new standing loop from Capri 0.8.6, facing either way, with its blink and glitches. With Legacy stand on, she wears it on her old stand instead.
