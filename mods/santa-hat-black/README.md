# Santa Hat: Black

A sharp black Santa hat for her, in every pose.

- Kind: skin
- Made by: Issei

The hat stays on her head when she stands, walks, sits, lies down, sleeps and sways to music, and it glitches along with her. The tip always trails behind her, so it flips when she turns around. Pick this one or the crimson one in the Mods window, since Capri only wears one skin at a time.
