# Galaxy Hair: Ember

Her hair as a galaxy: her red melting into plum and deep space.

- Kind: skin
- Made by: Issei

The galaxy is cut into flat, sharp shards to match her art, the little flecks in her hair become stars, and it moves with her in every pose. Only her hair changes, so her red arms and legs stay red. There are also Nebula and Aurora versions; Capri wears one skin at a time, so pick one in the Mods window.

It covers her new standing loop from Capri 0.8.6, facing either way, with its blink and glitches. With Legacy stand on, she wears it on her old stand instead.
