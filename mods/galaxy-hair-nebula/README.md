# Galaxy Hair: Nebula

Her hair as a galaxy: deep space with violet and pink clouds.

- Kind: skin
- Made by: Issei

The galaxy is cut into flat, sharp shards to match her art, the little flecks in her hair become stars, and it moves with her in every pose. Only her hair changes, so her red arms and legs stay red. There are also Ember and Aurora versions; Capri wears one skin at a time, so pick one in the Mods window.
