# Fire: Witchfire

Capri on fire, in green witchfire.

- Kind: skin
- Made by: Issei

Her hair burns from hot roots out to dark tips, with the flames licking outward along her spikes. Flames also rise off the top edges of her hair, arms and shoulders, so her outline flickers, and her body glows like embers just under them and cools to charcoal lower down. It all moves in every pose, and it glitches and flashes along with her. There are also Blaze and Bluefire versions; Capri wears one skin at a time, so pick one in the Mods window.
