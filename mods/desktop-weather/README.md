# Desktop Weather

Say "make it snow", "rain", "confetti" or "cherry blossoms" and it falls across your whole desktop.

- Kind: code mod
- Made by: Issei

| Say | She says |
|---|---|
| make it snow | Let it snow. |
| heavy rain | Downpour. Hope you brought an umbrella. |
| confetti! | Confetti! |
| a little cherry blossoms | A few petals on the breeze. |
| stop the snow | Clearing the sky. |

It covers every screen in a see-through layer, and your clicks go straight through, so you can keep working under it. Snow piles up on your taskbar and melts once you stop it. Confetti shoots out of both bottom corners. Confetti and petals land and lie there for a few seconds. Add "light" or "heavy" to change how much falls. On any screen where a fullscreen game or video is open, it hides until you leave fullscreen. After Capri restarts the sky is clear. Windows only.
